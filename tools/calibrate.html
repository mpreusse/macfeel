MacFeel calibration

Measures how far the cursor moves when the mouse moves a known distance, at three speeds. Do it on your Mac and on Ubuntu with the same mouse, then compare. Nothing leaves this page.

  1. Put a ruler on the desk. Use the same mouse (and the same mouse DPI setting) on both machines.
  2. For each speed, click Measure. The pointer is captured (it disappears).
  3. Hold the mouse with its edge at the ruler's 0 mark, move it straight right to the 10 cm mark at the requested speed, then click.
  4. Do each speed 2–3 times; the table averages them.

Scrolling

Put a mark on the scroll wheel (a bit of tape or a pen dot). For each row, click Measure, then turn the wheel half a turn downwards (by the mark) at the requested pace, and wait a second. If your wheel can spin freely (e.g. MX Master), switch it to the clicking (ratchet) mode first. Use the same browser on both machines: browsers differ in how far one notch scrolls.
